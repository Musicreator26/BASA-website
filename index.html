About BASA

From 1970 to Brunei Aquatics today

The Brunei Amateur Swimming Association was founded on 30 March 1970. In 2025, the organisation evolved its identity to Brunei Aquatics — reflecting our broader stewardship across all aquatic disciplines.

We are a proud member of:

  • World Aquatics — the global governing body
  • Asian Swimming Federation (AASF)
  • Southeast Asia Swimming Federation (SEASF)
