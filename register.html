Clubs & Swimmers Registration

Register your club for affiliation, or register a swimmer for the new season — securely via Google Forms.

Choose your form

Online registration with Brunei Aquatics

Both forms are hosted on Google Forms — secure, mobile-friendly, and routed straight to the BASA secretariat.

For athletes

Swimmer Registration

For all swimmers competing under any BASA-affiliated club for the new season.

  • Personal & contact details
  • IC / Passport identification
  • Affiliated club selection
  • Disciplines (swimming, diving, water polo, etc.)
  • Parent / guardian consent (if under 18)
  • Medical declaration & code of conduct agreement
Open Swimmer Form →

⏱ Approx. 5 minutes · Opens in a new tab

For clubs

Club Registration

For clubs applying for first-time affiliation or renewing affiliation with Brunei Aquatics.

  • Club name & year established
  • Training location(s)
  • Number of swimmers & coaches
  • Disciplines offered
  • Primary contact & head coach details
  • Constitution acknowledgement & safeguarding
Open Club Form →

⏱ Approx. 8 minutes · Opens in a new tab

💡

Need help with your registration?

Email contact@bruneiaquatics.com or contact your affiliated club directly. The BASA secretariat will respond within 3 working days.

How it works

From submission to confirmation

1

Submit form

Complete the appropriate Google Form and submit.

2

BASA review

The secretariat reviews your submission against eligibility criteria.

3

Email confirmation

You'll receive a confirmation email with next steps.

4

Active membership

Once approved, your registration is active for the season.
