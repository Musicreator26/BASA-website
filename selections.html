National Selections

A transparent and rigorous selection pathway for national swimmers and technical officials representing Brunei Darussalam.

Swimmers selection

A fair and performance-oriented pathway

Brunei Aquatics is committed to transparent and thorough selection of national swimmers for international competitions. Our process aims to ensure a fair and performance-oriented approach to assembling the national squad.

Selection considers not just past performances, but also versatility, consistency, and potential. The process is reviewed regularly to keep it aligned with the strategic goals of Brunei Aquatics.

How selection works

The three pillars of swimmer selection

1

Selection Criteria

Performance benchmarks set against published qualifying times, recent meet results, and FINA point standards relative to event and category.

2

Additional Considerations

Versatility across events, season-long consistency, training compliance, anti-doping standing, and developmental potential.

3

Selection Process

Trials → committee review → preliminary nomination → appeals window → ratification → official announcement.

Note: Specific qualifying times and the full selection policy are circulated to affiliated clubs before each competition cycle. Contact your club secretary or BASA directly for the latest documents.
Technical officials

Officiating at international standard

Technical officials are the backbone of fair, well-run competition. Brunei Aquatics maintains its own selection guidelines for officials representing Brunei at regional and international meets.
